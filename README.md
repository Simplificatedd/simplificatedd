# Hi, I'm Raven 👋

I grew up playing PC games on an old Pentium computer with a CRT monitor. Some games came in floppy disks, while the more expensive ones came in CDs. That was probably where my interest in computers started.

In primary school, I participated in Computer Skills Challenges and received a Distinction once. I later went into Electronics and Computer Engineering at Ngee Ann Polytechnic, where I picked up a mix of hardware and software skills.

Outside of school, this was also around the time I started learning how to build computers and playing around with different operating systems.

After an uneventful few years in the army, I couldn't get into any IT-related course at a local university. Private university wasn't really an option financially either, so I went to work at UOB as a Software Test Analyst.

There, I got exposed to the application development lifecycle, testing cycles, and the security measures that go into banking applications.

After working for about a year, I was offered a spot in SUSS' new full-time ICT programme, which I promptly accepted. Along with 3 other students, I founded our school's ICT Interest Group, and I was also awarded the SUSS Scholarship.

During my time at SUSS, I joined Deloitte as a Software Developer Intern, and later spent a semester at NUS through the Singapore Universities Student Exchange Programme (SUSEP).

And that’s roughly where I am now. I’m still grateful for the opportunities I’ve had along the way, especially at a time when AI is changing how quickly software is built, how people learn, and what it even means to be good at working with computers.

Still learning, still building things, and still playing around with computers.
